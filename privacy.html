Privacy Policy

Moonfleck Labs · Effective 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play games made by Moonfleck Labs ("we", "us"), currently Almost Regular Snake for Android. If you have any questions, write to moonfleck.labs@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for an account, your name, your email or any other personal details.
  • Your scores, achievements, settings and saved game stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a save file on your device with your best scores and their dates, achievements and play statistics, your settings (language, sound, vibration), a game in progress so you can continue it, and how many games have been played since the last ad. This file never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to continue a game. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalise, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads.

We don't receive this information in a form that identifies you; we only see summary reports (for example, how many ads were shown). Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can refuse; you will then see non-personalised ads. Clearing the game's data in Android settings shows the form again so you can change your choice.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Personalised ads: you can manage personalised ads in Google My Ad Center.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and the consent form.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in the game's menu.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

We don't run servers that store your data. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google using the links above, but you are always welcome to contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
